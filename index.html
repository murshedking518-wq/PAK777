Privacy Policy

Jetour Private Solution ke privacy policy mein khush aamdeed.

Hum aapki zaati maloomat ki hifazat karte hain. Ye policy batati hai ke hum kaunsi maloomat collect karte hain aur usay kaise istemal karte hain.

1. Maloomat Jo Hum Collect Karte Hain:

  • Email address (account banane ke liye)
  • Naam (optional)
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Wallet details (withdrawal ke liye)
  • Referral code aur referred by information

2. Maloomat Ka Istemal:

Hum aapki maloomat ko account manage karne, payments process karne, aur game ki services behtar banane ke liye istemal karte hain.

3. Maloomat Ki Hifazat:

Aapki maloomat Firebase secure servers par mehfooz hai. Hum kisi third party ko aapki zaati maloomat nahi bechte.

4. Cookies:

Hum cookies use karte hain taake aapka login session qaim rahe.

5. Aapke Huqooq:

Aap apni maloomat dekh sakte hain, update kar sakte hain, ya account delete kar sakte hain.

Agar koi sawaal ho to WhatsApp channel par raabta karein.
